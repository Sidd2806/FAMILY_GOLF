ABOUT US

Home to a 46-bay, multi-tier, floodlit driving range, powered by Toptracer Range technology. Complimented by a practice green and bunker, coffee shop and American Golf Store. There truly is something for everyone as we also boast two outdoor 18-hole dinosaur themed crazy golf courses.


Please note: we are a cashless venue. The range closes at 10pm with last balls at 9pm.

TOPTRACER RANGE

Experience cutting-edge golf technology with our Toptracer Range. Track your shots, play fun games, and improve your skills with real-time feedback and analytics.

ADVENTURE GOLF

Enjoy a fun-filled round of crazy golf with our dinosaur-themed adventure course. Perfect for families, friends, and team outings of all ages.

GOLF LESSONS

Take your golf game to the next level with professional coaching tailored for all skill levels. Our PGA-qualified instructors are here to help you improve.
